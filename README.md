
#  Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard  📊

An interactive Power BI dashboard developed to transform raw sales data into meaningful business insights. This project analyzes sales performance across 2022–2023, enabling stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify revenue trends, evaluate product performance, and support data-driven decision-making.

## Key Features

- Year-over-Year (YoY) Analysis to measure sales growth and performance changes between 2022 and 2023.
- Revenue & Sales KPIs with interactive visualizations for quick performance monitoring.
- Product Category Analysis to identify top-performing and underperforming categories.
- Regional Sales Insights to compare performance across different markets and uncover growth opportunities.
- Payment Method Analysis to understand customer purchasing behavior.
- Revenue vs. Quantity Analysis to evaluate sales volume and profitability trends.
- Interactive Filters & Drill-downs for dynamic exploration of business data.
